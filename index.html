About Us

InAmigos Foundation was founded on September 23, 2020, by Mr. Govind Shukla. It is a Section 8 registered non-profit organization, licensed by the Central Government and based in Chhattisgarh.

The foundation holds 80G & 12A certifications, CSR-1 registration, NITI Aayog NGO DARPAN registration and IAF ISO 9001:2015 certification. These recognitions show transparency, accountability and commitment to quality service.

InAmigos works through projects focused on food distribution, education, women empowerment, animal welfare, environmental conservation and youth development.
